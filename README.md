## ✨ About HUMBLE MUSIC

**HUMBLE MUSIC** is an Android music application that connects with your Spotify account and brings your music, playlists, and listening experience into a personalized interface.

Choose between two interfaces, enjoy your music, and make the experience your own.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🎵 **Spotify Login** — Connect using your Spotify account.
- 📚 **Playlists & Songs** — Access your Spotify playlists and music.
- 🚫 **Ad-Free Playback** — Enjoy uninterrupted playback.
- 🔊 **High-Quality Audio** — High-quality audio experience.
- 🖥️ **Classic UI** — A familiar Spotify-style interface.
- ✨ **HUMBLE UI** — A custom interface designed for HUMBLE MUSIC.
- 🔄 **Interface Switching** — Switch between different UI experiences.
- 📱 **Android Support** — Designed for Android devices.

---

## 🎨 Interfaces

### Classic UI

A familiar Spotify-style interface designed for a simple and recognizable music experience.

### HUMBLE UI

A custom-designed interface with its own visual identity, built around the HUMBLE MUSIC experience.

## ⚙️ Installation

1. Download the appropriate APK for your device.
2. Open the downloaded APK.
3. Allow installation from unknown sources if prompted.
4. Install HUMBLE MUSIC.
5. Open the application.
6. Log in using your Spotify account.
